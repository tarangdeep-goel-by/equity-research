## Holding Company Mode (Auto-Detected)

This is a holding/investment company. Its primary value derives from stakes in other listed entities, NOT from operating earnings.

**Primary Valuation: NAV (Net Asset Value) Discount/Premium**
- Calculate NAV: sum of market value of all listed holdings (shares x current price) + fair value of unlisted investments (Ind AS 109 requires fair value, not historical book value — use the Level 3 fair value disclosed in the standalone notes per Ind AS 113) + net cash
- Compare to current market cap -> holding company discount (typically 40-65%+ in India; the 20-40% range reflects global averages, not India — Indian holdcos like Bajaj Holdings, Tata Investment and Maharashtra Scooters routinely trade at 50-65%+ discounts to NAV)
- Narrowing discount = catalyst; widening = governance concern
- **SEBI special call-auction catalyst (Jun 2024):** SEBI's special call-auction mechanism (no price band) lets thinly-traded listed Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) — those with >=50% of assets in other listed companies' scrips and >=1yr listed — find fair price; first auction Oct 2024, annual thereafter on release of audited financials. Track eligibility and auction outcomes as the primary regulatory catalyst for discount narrowing.

**Metrics that give misleading results for holding companies:**
- PE ratio — earnings are mostly dividend income (FVOCI MTM gains bypass the P&L under Ind AS 109), not operating profit, so PE doesn't reflect business value
- EV/EBITDA — same issue; there is no meaningful operating EBITDA to value
- Revenue growth — holding companies have no operating revenue; "revenue"/PL figures reflect dividend timing only — note that under Ind AS 109 strategic equity stakes are typically elected at FVOCI, so MTM gains bypass the P&L entirely and flow to OCI/reserves (and are not recycled to P&L even on sale)
- ROCE — no meaningful capital employed in operations; the value lies in the portfolio of stakes

**Emphasize:** NAV discount trend, portfolio composition, dividend income yield, corporate governance (why does the discount exist?), demerger/simplification potential.
