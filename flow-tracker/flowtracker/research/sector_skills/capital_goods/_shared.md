## Capital Goods / Industrials Mode (Auto-Detected)

This is a capital goods, engineering, or industrial company. Order book visibility and execution drive valuations.

**Primary Metrics:**
- **Order Book / Order Inflow** — the forward revenue pipeline. Track Book-to-Bill ratio (order inflow / revenue). >1.0x = growing pipeline
- **Order Book / Revenue ratio** — execution visibility in years. Compare against peer median via `get_peer_sector(section='benchmarks')` and the company's own trend
- **Execution Run-Rate** — quarterly revenue as % of opening order book. Measures execution speed
- **Working Capital Cycle** — capital goods have long WC cycles (advance-heavy, receivable-heavy). Track CCC direction

**Revenue Quality:**
- **Fixed-price contracts & Price Variation Clauses (PVC)** — fixed-price = input cost risk, but Indian EPC/equipment contracts standardly protect margins via PVC linked to IEEMA price indices and the RBI WPI (escalation passed to the buyer), not by converting to cost-plus. Cost-plus is a distinct contract type. Check whether fixed-price orders are PVC-covered or genuinely lump-sum (the latter carries full RM-spike risk)
- **Domestic vs export mix** — export orders often have better margins but FX risk
- **Government vs private orders** — government orders = longer payment cycles but steady flow

**Valuation:** PE is primary. EV/EBITDA acceptable. DCF valid if order book provides cash flow visibility. Premium justified for companies with strong order book growth + improving margins.

**Make in India / Defence moat:**
- **Make in India (Class-I local supplier)** — government tenders mandate minimum local content under the Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order 2017: Class-I = ≥50% local content, Class-II = 20-50%. Class-I status is a critical regulatory moat for winning PSU/government orders (Class-I gets purchase preference; below-20% non-local suppliers are often excluded). Check local-content disclosures
- **Defence indigenisation** — for defence-exposed players, track exposure to MoD Positive Indigenisation Lists (PILs, now 5 lists covering thousands of items reserved for domestic sourcing) and the defence PLI scheme. Indigenisation mandates convert import substitution into a structural domestic order pipeline

**Emphasize:** Order inflow trajectory, book-to-bill, execution timelines, margin profile of new vs legacy orders, counterparty quality (government/PSU/private), and FX impact on export orders.
