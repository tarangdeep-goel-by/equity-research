## Merchant/Renewable Power Mode (Auto-Detected)

This is a merchant power producer or integrated utility with significant unregulated/renewable capacity.

**Key Framework:**
- Unlike regulated utilities, merchant power earnings are driven by power exchange prices, fuel costs, and PPA terms
- Revenue and EBITDA growth are meaningful for merchant producers (unlike regulated utilities where fuel passthrough distorts revenue)
- Standard valuation (PE, EV/EBITDA, DCF) is more applicable than for regulated utilities

**Primary Valuation Metrics:**
- **EV/EBITDA**: primary metric for merchant/renewable power
- **P/E**: acceptable for merchant producers with stable earnings
- **DCF**: valid if PPA mix and capacity pipeline are visible

**Emphasize:** Merchant vs PPA revenue mix (higher PPA % = more predictable), renewable portfolio % and growth, power exchange price trends, fuel cost position, capacity additions, and operating leverage from renewable transition (zero fuel cost).

**Risk Factors:** Power exchange price volatility (within CERC caps — ₹10/kWh standard DAM/RTM, ₹20/kWh HP-DAM), fuel supply risk (for thermal), regulatory changes (RPO obligations, the 40% Renewable Generation Obligation, green energy mandates), and discom counterparty risk on *current* dues (legacy dues largely cleared under the LPS Rules 2022).

**Structural shifts to track (2024-26):**
- **Market coupling.** CERC is rolling out market coupling, starting with the Day-Ahead Market (phased from Jan 2026, operated via Grid-India / round-robin across exchanges). It unifies price discovery into a single uniform clearing price across all exchanges — this compresses inter-exchange arbitrage, narrows IEX's volume/price advantage, and changes how merchant realizations are modeled. Treat post-coupling DAM as one coupled market, not exchange-specific prices.
- **General Network Access (GNA).** The CERC Connectivity & GNA Regulations, 2022 replaced the legacy Open Access / Long-Term-Access framework for the inter-state grid. Evaluate merchant and renewable grid connectivity, transmission charges, and short-term access (Temporary GNA) under GNA — not legacy Open Access terminology.
- **Renewable Generation Obligation (RGO).** Coal/lignite plants commissioned after 1 Apr 2025 face a 40% RGO (RE capacity or equivalent RE procurement), but the MoP's Aug 2025 clarification made the *means of compliance* effectively voluntary (RE can sit in a wholly-owned subsidiary/JV). This drives thermal-heavy IPPs' capital allocation toward captive RE — assess it when modeling capex.
